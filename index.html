Margin — privacy and support

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Margin is a personal budget for entering income and bills manually. It is sold as a £4.99 one-time purchase by Sam Gates (Individual).

Privacy at a glance

  • Margin does not connect to your bank or import transactions.
  • Margin does not require an account, login, cloud account, or social sign-in.
  • Margin has no ads, analytics, tracking, or third-party measurement SDK.
  • The ledger is kept locally on your device for the app’s core experience.
  • Margin does not sell, rent, or share your ledger data for advertising.
  • Backups are user-controlled files made when you choose Download a copy. They are not silently uploaded by Margin.

What you enter

You may enter information such as income, bills, amounts, dates, categories, notes, arrears, and finite bill occurrences. This information is used to calculate and display Margin’s local budget views, including Leftover, The Run, The Ask, This month, Left to pay, and Arrears.

Where it is stored

The working ledger is stored locally in the app’s storage on your device. The app does not operate a remote ledger or require a network connection for its core budget experience. If you export a backup, the resulting file is controlled by you and may be stored wherever you choose, including a location or service outside Margin’s control. Treat exported files as sensitive and delete or protect them when no longer needed.

If you delete the app or clear its app data, locally stored ledger data may be removed. Keep a backup if you need one.

Sharing and third parties

Margin does not send your ledger to Sam Gates, advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers. The operating system may show its own standard file/share interfaces when you save or share a backup; any destination you choose is governed by that destination’s terms and privacy policy.

Margin may depend on Apple’s App Store and iOS services for installation, updates, and device operation. Apple handles those services under Apple’s own terms. Margin does not add an advertising or tracking SDK.

Children

Margin is a general personal budgeting tool and is not directed at children. Do not enter another person’s sensitive information unless you have a lawful reason and permission to do so.

Security and limits

Local storage reduces the amount of data sent away, but no device or file can be guaranteed perfectly secure. Use your device passcode and protect any downloaded backup. Margin is not a bank, financial adviser, or transaction feed; its calculations are based on what you type.
